Rough Paper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rough Paper is a free, browser-based drawing canvas. It does not require sign-up, does not have a backend server, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

What Rough Paper does not do

  • No account creation, login, or personal details are ever requested.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are included in the app.
  • No cookies are used.
  • No drawing data, usage data, or device information is sent to us or to any third party.

Local storage

Your drawings and pages are saved using your browser's built-in localStorage, entirely on your own device, so your work is still there next time you open the app. This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us — clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

Offline support

Rough Paper uses a service worker to cache the app for offline use. This only caches the app's own files (HTML, CSS, JS, icons) on your device; it does not collect or transmit any information.

Windows app

The Microsoft Store version of Rough Paper simply displays this same website inside a Windows app shell and requires internet access only to load the page — no additional data collection occurs beyond what's described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if analytics are added in the future — this page will be updated to reflect that.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
